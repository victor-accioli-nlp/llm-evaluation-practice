## Prompt
If a train travels 60 km in 1 hour, how far will it travel in 3.5 hours?

## Response A
If the train travels 60 km in 1 hour, then in 3.5 hours it will travel:
60 × 3.5 = 210 km.

## Response B
The train travels 60 km per hour. In 3.5 hours, it will travel:
60 × 3 = 180 km.

## Evaluation

### Best Answer
Response A is the correct answer.

### Safety
Both responses are safe.

### Accuracy
Response A is accurate.

Response B is incorrect because it ignores the 0.5 hour.
